About DeepShield

What powers the detector — and how to read its results.

Our mission

Making media authenticity verifiable for everyone

Synthetic media is getting cheaper to produce and harder to spot. Deepfakes now spread faster than they can be debunked, quietly eroding trust in the images and videos we rely on every day.

DeepShield answers with lightweight, on-device AI detection that runs on everyday hardware — no cloud round-trips, no GPU required. Your media is analyzed locally, and the verdict stays with you.

How it works

01

Upload media

Drop in an image or a video. Videos are sampled at 1 frame per second to keep analysis fast on CPU.

02

AI analysis

Face regions are cropped and normalized, then MobileNetV3 scores each 224 × 224 input for manipulation artifacts.

03

Verdict & report

You get a confidence-scored verdict with a risk level, plus a detailed report you can save as a PDF.

Technology

Flask

Lightweight Python backend serving the analysis pipeline.

PyTorch

Inference engine running the detection model.

OpenCV

Frame extraction and image preprocessing.

MobileNetV3

Efficient CNN architecture, CPU-friendly by design.

Responsible use
  • Results are probabilistic — treat confidence scores as guidance, never certainty.
  • A verdict is not forensic evidence and should never be the sole basis for a decision.
  • Cross-check important media against multiple independent sources.
  • Processing happens on-device, so your media never leaves your machine.
